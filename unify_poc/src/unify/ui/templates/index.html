Cross-tool consistency · proof of concept

When the design moves, the ticket and the code should know.

Unify reads events from Figma, Git & Jira, links the same work across tools, and tells the right person — in Slack and on the dashboard — when something has drifted out of sync.

How the pipeline works

Source data

Raw records as they arrive from each tool. Click a card to locate it in the graph and trace its links. Slack shows where alerts are delivered.

Relationship graph

Nodes are entities, columns are tools, lines are links. Click a node to inspect it; click an alert to light up its conflict chain.
